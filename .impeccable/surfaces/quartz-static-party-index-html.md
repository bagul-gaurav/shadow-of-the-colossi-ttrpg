## Scope

Party Journal and Quests app (one file, two views via #journal / #quests), embedded in the campaign wiki. Mode: Operate. Users: the party and GM, at the table on phones and between sessions on laptops. Fastest path: jotting a journal entry. Avoid: corporate/SaaS feel. Terms: Journal, entry, Quests.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The party's record is a surveyor's sheet of Ecerah: entries are field notes, quests are plotted survey marks. Refuses the notes-app sidebar and SaaS checklist.
OWN-WORLD: Pale grey-green survey ground, near-black ink, contour brown for terrain and meta, hydro blue for the party and assignees, magenta overprint only for the one primary action. Hairline rules, title-block label grid (ref, title, author, date), SVG triangle survey marks, contours only in margins and empty ground. Dark: night chart, deep slate ground.
STORY: A player sees the party's latest notes, taps New entry and writes, then ticks quests.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width title block: sheet name, scale bar, surveyor, magenta New entry right. Below at 760px+: legend index (34%) beside open field note; phones stack.
FORM: Survey of the Colossi, #3 of 7; seed 8a7e8098. Signature: ticking a quest inks its survey mark.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
